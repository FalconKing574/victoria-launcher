Both files here are the same variable font (weight 300-800), split by character
range the way Google Fonts serves it:

  outfit-latin.woff2      basic Latin, covers Spanish accents and punctuation
  outfit-latin-ext.woff2  extended Latin, for names the pack may contain

They are bundled rather than fetched at runtime so the launcher opens with no
network at all. See src/renderer/src/theme/tokens.css.
